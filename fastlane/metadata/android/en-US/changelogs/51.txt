A large release built from the first wave of feedback.

New:
* Work profiles and private space are now supported; a locked private space hides its apps until you unlock it.
* Several widgets, swiped between one at a time, with page dots that fade away.
* A search box in the app list, at the top or the bottom of the screen.
* Sort each letter by how often you open its apps.
* Swipe up from the home screen to open the app list, and swipe left or right below your favorites to launch an app.
* Long-press the wallpaper for add favorites, add widget, edit layout and settings.
* Alignment and icon side, set separately for the favorites and the app list, now including centered.
* An option to draw no icons at all.
* Set how far the A-Z strip reaches, from edit layout.
* Configurable edge swipe width and status bar timeout.
* A short welcome for new installs, whose favorites now start centered on the screen.

Fixed:
* The widget's menu opens at your finger instead of below the widget.
* Editing an app's name no longer loses it when you go on to change the icon.
* Double-tap to lock moved to the A-Z strip, and dismissing the list is no longer delayed waiting for a second tap.
* Opening and closing the app list now take the same push, and a pull straight after a flick is no longer swallowed.
* The app list no longer draws under the A-Z strip.
* The launcher no longer appears in its own app list.
* Edit layout uses steppers rather than drag handles, reorders from a grab handle, and the back button leaves it.
