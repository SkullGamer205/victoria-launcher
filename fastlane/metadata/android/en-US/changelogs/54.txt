New:
* Search can find apps you have hidden from the list, so they stay out of the way but are still reachable by name. Off by default, under the search bar setting.
* Hiding the status bar is now asked separately for the home screen and for the app list.

Fixed:
* The search field no longer draws a dark panel of its own over the app list; it matches the background whatever the wallpaper dim is set to.
* The list now fades at both ends rather than only the top, and only where something is actually scrolled past, so the first and last rows stay crisp at rest.
* With the search field at the bottom, the keyboard no longer pushes the results off the top of the screen.
* Each search starts at the top of its own results instead of wherever the list was left.
* The app list no longer shifts when the status bar fades away, no longer scrolls underneath it, and the bar no longer flashes into view when the list opens.
* Tightened the gap above the first letter, and set the settings shortcut apart from the last app above it.
