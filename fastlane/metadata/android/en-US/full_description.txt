Victoria Launcher is a minimal, list-based Android home screen, and an open source alternative to Niagara Launcher.

The home screen keeps a short list of favorites under a clock and weather widget. Everything else lives in an alphabetical app list you reach by swiping the edge of the screen, with an A-Z scrubber for jumping straight to a letter.

Layout, alignment, icon pack, icon and text size, spacing, font and colors are all adjustable. Widgets, an optional search box, an optional Now Playing block, and work profiles and private space are all supported.

No ads, no tracking, no network access.
